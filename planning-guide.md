# ADHD Planning Guide

A simple guide to planning your day and week with the free files in this repository.

## Step 1: Pick 3 priorities

Every morning, pick three tasks that would make the day feel complete if nothing else got done. Write them in `daily-checklist.md`.

- Keep the list short — three is enough.
- Make the first one small, so it is easy to start.
- If everything feels urgent, pick the one that has a deadline first.

## Step 2: Use the daily checklist

Open `daily-checklist.md` each morning.

1. Write your top 3 priorities.
2. Follow the morning routine.
3. Work in short blocks: 25 minutes of work, 5 minutes of break.
4. Do a 5-minute evening reset: tick what got done and write tomorrow's top 3.

## Step 3: Plan the week on Sunday

Open `weekly-checklist.md` once a week, for about 10 minutes.

1. Write one line of focus for the week.
2. Pick your top 3 priorities for the week.
3. Give each day one small step.
4. At the end of the week, review what worked and write next week's top 3.

## Step 4: Track 3 habits

Open `habit-tracker.md` and pick three small habits.

- Keep each habit small enough to do every day.
- Do it at the same time each day, if you can.
- Tick the box each day you do it.
- A missed day is fine — start fresh the next morning.

## Step 5: When the plan breaks

Plans break. That is normal.

- A missed day is not a failed week — start again the next morning.
- If a task keeps moving to tomorrow, make it smaller or split it in two.
- If the list feels heavy, cut it back to one task and do that first.
- If a habit keeps failing, make it smaller or move it to another time.

## A simple week, step by step

1. **Sunday (10 minutes):** open the weekly planner, set the focus and top 3.
2. **Each morning (5 minutes):** open the daily checklist, write the top 3.
3. **During the day:** work in 25-minute blocks with 5-minute breaks.
4. **Each evening (5 minutes):** reset — tick what got done, write tomorrow's top 3.
5. **End of the week (1 minute):** review the habit tracker and the weekly review.
